# VPTC Fees and Scholarships

Vignesh Polytechnic College provides affordable, high-quality technical education.

## Q: What are the fees at VPTC? What is the fee structure?

**Total Annual Fee for all courses: ₹42,000 per year.**

### Semester-wise Payment Breakdown (General Category):
| Semester | Amount |
|---|---|
| 1st Semester (Odd) | ₹20,000 |
| 2nd Semester (Even) | ₹22,000 |
| **Total per year** | **₹42,000** |

- All courses (CSE, ECE, EEE, Mechanical, Civil, S&H) have the same fee: ₹42,000/year.
- Transport fee is included within the ₹42,000 total fee.

---

## Q: What are the fees for SC/ST students? Are there scholarships for SC/ST?

**SC/ST students pay ₹35,000 per year (₹7,000 discount vs. general).**

### SC/ST Semester-wise Breakdown:
| Semester | College Fee | Pocket Money Given Back | Net Student Payment |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1st Semester (Odd) | ₹15,000 | — | ₹15,000 |
| 2nd Semester (Even) | ₹20,000 | ₹2,500 returned to student | ₹22,500 (₹22,500 paid, ₹2,500 returned) |
| **Total per year** | **₹35,000** | **₹2,500 pocket money** | **₹35,000** |

---

## Q: What other fees are charged?

### One-Time & Semester Fees:
| Fee Type | Amount |
|---|---|
| Admission Fee (one-time only) | ₹100 |
| Exam Fee (per semester) | ₹750 |
| Lab Fee (per semester) | ₹20 |

---

## Q: Can I pay fees in installments?

Yes. Fees can be paid:
- **Lump sum** (full year at once)
- **Semester-wise** (split into two payments per year)

---

## Q: How do I apply for a scholarship?

Submit the following at the time of admission:
1. **Income Certificate**
2. **Community Certificate**
3. **"Aadidravidar Scholarship" form**

All eligible SC/ST students are entitled to the government scholarship scheme facilitated directly by the college.
