# VPTC Fees and Scholarships

Vignesh Polytechnic College provides affordable, high-quality technical education.

## Q: What are the fees at VPTC? What is the fee structure?

**The annual fee is ₹42,000 for ALL departments and ALL courses at VPTC.**

This applies to:
- Computer Science Engineering (CSE)
- Electronics and Communication Engineering (ECE)
- Electrical and Electronics Engineering (EEE)
- Mechanical Engineering
- Civil Engineering
- Science and Humanities (S&H / 1st Year)

There is NO difference in fees between departments. All courses cost ₹42,000 per year.

## Semester-wise Payment Summary (General Category - All Departments):
| Semester | Amount |
|---|---|
| 1st Semester (Odd) | ₹20,000 |

## Q: What are the fees for SC/ST students? Are there scholarships for SC/ST?

**SC/ST students pay ₹35,000 per year for ALL departments (₹7,000 less than general).**

### SC/ST Semester-wise Breakdown (All Departments):
| Semester | Fee Paid | Pocket Money Returned | Net College Fee |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1st Semester (Odd) | ₹15,000 | — | ₹15,000 |
| 2nd Semester (Even) | ₹22,500 | ₹2,500 returned to student | ₹20,000 |
| **Total per year** | **₹37,500** | **₹2,500 back** | **₹35,000** |

## Q: Can I pay fees in installments?

Yes. Fees can be paid:
- **Lump sum** (full year at once)
- **Semester-wise** (₹20,000 in odd semester + ₹22,000 in even semester)

---

## Q: How do I apply for a scholarship?

Submit the following at the time of admission:
1. **Income Certificate**
2. **Community Certificate**
3. **"Aadidravidar Scholarship" form**

All eligible SC/ST students are entitled to the government scholarship scheme facilitated directly by the college.
