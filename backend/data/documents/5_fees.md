# VPTC Fees and Scholarships

VPTC provides affordable, high-quality technical education.

## Fee Structure
* **Tuition Fees:** Fees are extremely affordable as per government norms. The Fee for all courses are ₹42,000 per year. The fees are can pay in each semster Rs.20,000 in first semseter and Rs.22,000 in second semester.

* **Transport Fees:** The transport fee is taken from the actual college fees(Rs.42,000).

## Government Scholarships & Schemes
VPTC facilitates full access to government scholarships for eligible students:

* **SC/ST Scholarships:** The Fee for SC & ST students are ₹35,000 per year. The fees are giving sapratly for each semester. 15,000 for first semester(odd semester) and 22,500 for second semester(even semester) for each year. The Rs.2,500 will given to the students from the second semster fee Rs.22,500 and balance Rs.20,000 is for college fees.*

## How to Apply for Scholarships
Submit income certificate and community certificate with a "Aadidravidar Scholarship" form at the time of admission.
