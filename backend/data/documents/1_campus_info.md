# Vignesh Polytechnic College (VPTC) - Campus Information

## About Us & History
Vignesh Polytechnic College, a premier Institution of high repute, was started in the year 1995 to impart quality Technical Education to the students of Tiruvannamalai District and around. Started with three disciplines of Engineering namely, Mechanical Engineering, Electrical and Electronics Engineering, and Electronics and Communication Engineering, the Institution has grown and at present, diploma courses in six disciplines of Engineering are being offered. The total intake in the first year is 360.

## Sree Selvavinayagar Trust
Tiruvannamalai is rich in heritage and culture, but the District is industrially and economically a backward District where the main occupation is agriculture. For the upliftment of the District, Education is a key factor. With this sole motive in mind, Thiru R. Kuppusamy formed the **Sree Selvavinayagar Trust**, a Charitable Trust for the cause of Education. Thiru R. Kuppusamy serves as the Chairman of the Trust and Board of Directors. 

* **Trust Address:** #262, Sesathri madam Street, Tiruvannamalai, 606 603, Tamilnadu.
* **Trust Email:** sreeselvavinyagart@gmail.com

Vignesh Polytechnic College was the first Institution given birth by the Trust in 1995. Within two years of inception, the College built its own serene campus with state-of-the-art Buildings, Laboratories, and Workshops. The Institution was ISO 9001:2008 certified by DNV up to April 2015. Over time, the Trust expanded into other fields of education, establishing the Vignesh Teacher Training Institute, Vignesh College of Education, Vignesh Nursing College, and Vignesh International School, forming the Vignesh Group of Institutions.

## Vision & Mission
* **Our Vision:** We, at Vignesh Polytechnic College impart Futuristic Technical Education as per curriculum with due importance to Practical oriented training based on the needs of the industries. We with a team of dedicated staff instill high standards of discipline in our students thus making them technologically superior and ethically strong. By virtue of this they will strive every nerve to improve the quality of life with ethics and contribute for the development of the society.
* **Our Mission:** Our mission is to provide quality Technical Education in a highly disciplined atmosphere with ethical and moral values to the students from all over Tamilnadu and especially to those from local and rural areas. We, with a dedicated team of staff, provide students individual attention, high quality education, enough practical training and take care of their character building to ensure improvement in the living standards of their families and contribute for the development of Industries and society.
