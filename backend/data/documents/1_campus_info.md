# Vignesh Polytechnic College (VPTC) - Campus Information

## General Overview
Vignesh Polytechnic College is a reputed institution established in 1995 to provide quality technical education to students in Tiruvannamalai district and nearby areas. It initially started with three engineering disciplines—Mechanical Engineering, Electrical and Electronics Engineering, and Electronics and Communication Engineering—and has since expanded to offer diploma courses in five engineering fields. The college was founded under the Sree Selvavinayagar Trust and was its first institution. Within two years, it developed its own well-equipped campus with modern buildings, laboratories, and workshops. The institution is led by its chairman, Thiru R. Kuppusamy (Mani), a visionary leader whose dedication and passion for education have significantly contributed to its growth. The college is known for its disciplined environment, strong academic performance, and well-qualified faculty. It was also ISO 9001:2008 certified until April 2015.
