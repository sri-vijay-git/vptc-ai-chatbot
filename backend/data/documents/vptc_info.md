## About Sree Selvavinayagar Trust
Tiruvannamalai is rich in heritage and culture, still the District is industrially and economically a backward District. The main occupation of the people is agriculture. For the upliftment of the District, Education is one of the key factors. With this sole motive in mind, Thiru R. Kuppusamy has formed Sree Selvavinayagar Trust, a Charitable Trust for the cause of Education. Vignesh Polytechnic College was the first Institution established by the Trust in 1995. Within two years of inception, the College started owning its own serene campus with state of the art Buildings, Laboratories and Workshops.

## Admission Details

### Eligibility
**NO AGE LIMIT** for any admission.

#### First Year (Semester 1)
- Pass in SSLC (10th) or equivalent examination recognized by the Board of Secondary Education, Tamil Nadu.

#### Lateral Entry - Direct 2nd Year (Semester 3)
- **Vocational Stream:** Students who have passed Higher Secondary Course (Vocational stream), considered based on total marks obtained in +2 examination.
- **Academic Stream:** Students who have passed Higher Secondary Course (Academic stream), considered based on total marks obtained in +2 examination.
- **ITI Stream:** 10th passed candidates who have qualified 2-year ITI courses are eligible for lateral entry to their respective courses.

#### Students from Other Boards
Students who studied in other state boards must produce:
- Migration Certificate
- Certificate of Permanent Residence
- Original Mark list, Transfer Certificate/Course Certificate and Conduct Certificate

### Documents Required for Admission
- 10th Mark Sheet (SSLC)
- Transfer Certificate (TC)
- Community Certificate
- Passport Size Photos
- Conduct Certificate
- Migration Certificate (for other board students)
- Permanent Residence Certificate (for other board students)
