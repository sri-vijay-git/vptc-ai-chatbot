# VPTC Admissions Guide

VPTC provides accessible technical education with straightforward admission procedures for students from all backgrounds.

## Eligibility & Age Limit
* **Age Limit:** NO AGE LIMIT.
* **Admission to First Semester (1st Year):** A pass in the S.S.L.C. Examination (10th standard) of the Board of Secondary Education, Tamilnadu, or any other Examination recognized as equivalent.

## Lateral Entry (Direct Second Year / Third Semester)
* **Vocational Stream Candidates:** Students who have passed the Higher Secondary Course (+2) Vocational stream will be considered, based on total marks obtained.
* **Academic Stream Candidates:** Students who have passed the Higher Secondary Course (+2) Academic stream will be considered, based on total marks obtained.
* **ITI Stream Candidates:** 10th passed candidates who have qualified in 2 years ITI courses are eligible for Lateral entry admission to the second year Diploma course matching their respective ITI trades.

## Required Documents for Admission
To finalize admission, all candidates must bring the original and photocopies of the following documents:
1. 10th Standard Mark Sheet (Original Mark list)
2. Transfer Certificate (TC) / Course Certificate
3. Conduct Certificate
4. Community Certificate (if applicable)

*Note: The applicant must come along with their Parent / Guardian for Admission.*

## Students From Other Boards (Outside Tamil Nadu)
Students who studied in other state boards should produce the following **additional** certificates alongside the standard documents above:
1. Migration Certificate.
2. Certificate of Permanent Residence.

## AICTE Approval & Anti-Ragging
* **AICTE Approval:** All courses offered at Vignesh Polytechnic College are strictly approved by the AICTE, New Delhi. (Approvals from 1995 to the current 2024-2025 academic year).
* **Anti-Ragging:** Ragging is strictly treated as an offence on campus.
