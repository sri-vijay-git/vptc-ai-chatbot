# VPTC Departments and Faculty

VPTC offer 5 focused departments for its 3-year Diploma Engineering programs. 

## 1. Civil Engineering
* **Head of Department (HOD):** Mr.S.Subash(Qualification: ME.civil) Experience: 15 years
* **Key Faculty:** Mis. S. Savithiri(Qualification: BE.civil)

### Infrastructure & Facilities
The Civil Engineering Dept. is equipped with all necessary laboratories for the Odd and Even semesters. Facilities include the Material Testing Lab with high capacity UTM, Surveying Lab with the latest equipment, CAD in Civil Engineering Lab, Construction practical lab, and Hydraulics and Plumbing lab. 
Students also undergo computer application training and English Communication Lab training. This 100% practical training ensures students are highly sought after by industries, maintaining a continuous 100% placement record.
* **Syllabus:** [M-Scheme (Download)](https://vigneshpolytechniccollege.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/06/Civil.pdf)
