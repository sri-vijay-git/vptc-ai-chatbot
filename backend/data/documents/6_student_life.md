# Campus Life, Facilities, and Placements at VPTC

## Placements and Career Services
A full-fledged **Placement Cell** with an Administrative Officer (AO) functioning as the **Placement Officer** operates in the college. The Placement Cell takes complete care of placing final year students in various organizations. Students opting for higher studies are excluded; all others are fully supported by the Placement Cell.

The institution has maintained a **steady record of 100% placements**. The performance of students in companies and feedback from stakeholders are continuously tracked. As a corporate social responsibility, the Placement Cell arranges a **"Mega Job Mela"** for students from other institutions every year since 1995, with overwhelming participation from industries, companies, and public/private sector undertakings.

* **Placement Officer / Administrative Officer (AO):** Mr. Raja
* **Placement Percentage:** 100% (for students opting for placement)

## Co-curricular & Extra Curricular Activities

### Students’ Association
Every department has an active Students’ Association with faculty advisors. They organize frequent guest lectures, industrial visits, and competitions. Their grandest event is the **Annual Technical Symposium**, which provides a platform for students from Polytechnic Colleges all over Tamilnadu to present papers on modern engineering developments.

### National Service Scheme (NSS)
To inculcate a mindset of serving society, VPTC operates two units of the NSS guided by two Programme Officers. Students volunteer in socio-economic development and conduct a 7-day Annual NSS camp involving road laying, canal cleaning, and hosting medical, dental, veterinary, and blood donation camps. The NSS unit also adopts a local village to render periodic services.

### Indian Society for Technical Education (ISTE)
The ISTE student chapter has functioned at VPTC for over ten years. It arranges Technical Seminars, Symposiums, Quiz Programmes, Guest Lectures, and career guidance. Students can become members by submitting an application and a nominal membership fee of Rs.150/-.

### Red Ribbon Club
The Red Ribbon Club operates actively with the core objective of personality development, youth awareness, and creating AIDS awareness among youngsters.

### Physical Education
Physical Training is fully integrated into extracurricular activities to keep students physically fit and mentally alert, with multiple adequate facilities provided for various games and sports.

## Institutional Committees & Policies
VPTC maintains strict guidelines to ensure a safe, disciplined, and supportive environment for all students and staff. The college has established the following official committees:

*   **Anti-Ragging Committee:** Ragging is strictly prohibited and treated as a serious criminal offence on campus. The institution enforces a zero-tolerance policy in compliance with AICTE and government regulations. [View Committee Details](https://vigneshpolytechniccollege.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/04/Anti-rag.pdf)

*   **Internal Complaint Committee (ICC):** Established to address and resolve internal concerns securely. [View Details](https://vigneshpolytechniccollege.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/04/ICC.pdf)
*   **SC / ST Committee (Prevention of Atrocities):** Dedicated to promoting an inclusive environment and preventing discrimination. [View Details](https://vigneshpolytechniccollege.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/04/SC-ST.pdf)
