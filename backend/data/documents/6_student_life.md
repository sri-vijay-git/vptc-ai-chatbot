# Campus Life, Facilities, and Placements at VPTC

## Placements and Career Services
VPTC has an active Training and Placement Cell focused on directly transitioning students into successful industrial jobs upon graduation.
* **Placement Officer:** Mr. Raja  
* **Administrative Officer(AO)** Mr. Raja 
* **Training Activities:** The placement cell arranges a “Mega Job Mela” every year since 1995 for the benefit of students. They take complete care of placements in various leading organizations.
* **Top Recruiting Companies:** Students are regularly placed in prominent organizations including **Sundaram Fasteners, Customised Technologies, Brakes India Ltd, Lucas-TVS, India Nippon Electrical Ltd, Super Auto Forge, Castwell Auto Parts, and Thai Summit Neel**.
* **Placement Percentage:** The institution has maintained a steady record of 100% placements for students opting for jobs.
